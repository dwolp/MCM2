# OpenMCM2 1.0.0 (Daniel's fork)

The first release of this fork of
[OpenMCM2](https://github.com/crspytopgn/openmcm2), built on OpenMCM2 0.2.1.
You need your own copy of Motocross Madness 2; see the
[README](https://github.com/dwolp/MCM2#readme) for what each change does.

Download the archive for your computer below, unpack it and start the
launcher (`mm2-launcher`). Its update check follows this fork's releases.

## Fixes

* **Difficulty Level in single events** counts: before, every single event
  was raced on Normal whatever was picked.
* **Cleaner starts:** in National and Supercross races the computer riders
  no longer pile into each other off the gate.
* **Track records are saved:** best laps, stunt scores and race times.
* **Enduro gates** count from either side, as in the original.
* **No white line** across the ground in the distance.
* **3D grass** (enhanced look) grows only on green ground, not on dirt,
  sand and rock.
* **Display Resolution** in Options > Graphics works, and the frame rate
  (F2) shows full screen too.

## The Tweaks box

Options, then **Tweaks...**, holds this fork's options; point at one to see
what it does.

* **Difficulty:** Easy, Normal and Hard set from 1 to 10 each: the other
  riders' speed and skill, how easily you crash, and their Quarry tricks.
  The original's are 3, 5 and 7; 10 is far harder than Hard.
* **Air steering** and **Air braking** sliders: bend or shorten your flight
  in the air (far left is the original game).
* **Quick get-up:** G or a pad's Back puts you back on the bike after a
  crash.
* **Modernised HUD**, **live gap to your best lap**, **race display nearer
  the middle** on ultrawide screens.
* **Steadier camera**, **field of view**, **controller vibration** with a
  strength slider, **VSync** and **show frame rate**.

## Also

* **Ultrawide** screens up to 21:9, with black bars beyond.
* **Play without the launcher:** the game starts with the launcher's
  settings, and a button makes a "Play MM2" desktop shortcut.
* **One-lap** National and Supercross races.
